From March 1, 2026, DataHub pricing becomes simpler and more granular.

## Compute
Compute units are now billed per second instead of per started minute. Short notebook sessions and quick pipeline activities cost less as a result.

## Pipeline activity runs
Pipeline activity runs are billed per 1,000 runs. The first 5,000 activity runs each month are included in every paid plan.

## Free tier
The free tier includes 5 GB of storage and one workspace. It is intended for learning and small proofs of concept.
