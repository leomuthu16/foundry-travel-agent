DataHub 2026.5 brings better visibility into pipeline runs and more free storage.

## Pipelines
Pipelines can now send run alerts by email or to a Microsoft Teams channel through a webhook when a run fails or succeeds. The maximum number of automatic retries per activity is raised from 3 to 5.

## Notebooks
You can now schedule a notebook directly from the notebook editor, without creating a pipeline.

## Pricing
Free tier storage increases from 5 GB to 10 GB.
