DataHub 2026.10 adds run history export, pipeline templates and an update to the FTP connector timeline.

## Pipelines
You can now export pipeline run history from the monitoring hub to a CSV file, filtered by date range and status. A new template gallery offers ready-made pipelines for common tasks such as copying data between storage accounts.

## Connectors
Based on customer feedback, removal of the legacy FTP connector moves from 2026.11 to 2027.1, giving teams more time to migrate to SFTP.
